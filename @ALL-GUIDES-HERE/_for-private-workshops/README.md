## Quick Start (3 Minutes)

1. **Pick your department** from the table above
2. **Run the "Learn from Context" prompt** (`01-learn-from-context.md` in your department folder)
3. **Drop 3 samples** into `B-brain/02-my-samples/_deluxe/[your-dept]/`
4. **Run "Learn from Samples"** (`02-learn-from-samples.md`)
5. **Create your first output** (`03-create-output.md`)

That's it. You're live.

Think of it as: base = the operating system. Deluxe = apps for your job.
