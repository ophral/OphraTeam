# HR Before / After

> What a real person in this department gets BEFORE the system vs. AFTER.

---

## The Situation

Sarah is the HR Director at a 50-person Series A SaaS company. The CEO just confirmed a reorg: 3 people are being let go, and the Customer Success and Support teams are merging into one. She has 90 minutes to draft the all-hands announcement before the leadership team reviews it. The wrong tone here is the difference between people understanding the decision and people losing trust in the company.

---

## ❌ Before: What They Wrote Without the System

> **Subject: Organizational Update**
>
> Team,
>
> I'm writing to share an important organizational update. As part of our ongoing efforts to streamline operations and position the company for sustainable growth, we have made the difficult decision to make some changes to our team structure.
>
> Effective immediately, we will be consolidating certain functions to drive greater efficiency and alignment across the business. Unfortunately, this means that we will be parting ways with a small number of valued team members. We are deeply grateful for their contributions and wish them the very best in their next chapter.
>
> In addition, the Customer Success and Support teams will be coming together as one unified team to better serve our customers.
>
> We understand that change can be challenging, and we want to assure you that these decisions were not made lightly. Our leadership team has been working hard to ensure that we are well positioned for the road ahead.
>
> If you have any questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to your manager or HR.
>
> Thank you for your continued dedication and hard work.
>
> Best,
> Sarah

Generic. Could be from any company. The phrase "parting ways with a small number of valued team members" is the kind of sentence that makes people angry at the messenger. No timing. No mention of severance. No acknowledgment of what people actually want to know: am I safe, what happens next, and was this handled with care.

---

## ✅ After: What the System Produced

> **Subject: A hard decision, and what it means for the team**
>
> Team,
>
> I want to be direct with you because you deserve that.
>
> Today we let three people go: Marcus from Sales Ops, Priya from Marketing, and David from Support. They are good people who did good work. The decision was not about performance. It was about runway, and where we need to focus.
>
> Here is the honest version. Our Series A gives us 18 months at the current burn. To extend that to 24 months and double down on the enterprise segment (where our last six closed deals came from), we made cuts in functions that supported the SMB motion we are stepping back from. That is the trade we made.
>
> Severance: 8 weeks pay, 3 months of health coverage, and accelerated equity vesting through end of Q3. Marcus, Priya, and David already know. They will be in the office through Friday if you want to say goodbye in person.
>
> The second change: Customer Success and Support are merging into one Customer Experience team under Jordan. Same headcount on that team. The goal is one owner per customer instead of two handoffs. Jordan will share the new structure on Thursday.
>
> What I am asking from you this week:
>
> - If you are worried about your role, talk to your manager. We are not planning more cuts.
> - If you want to understand the strategy shift, join the AMA tomorrow at 2pm. I will answer anything.
> - If you want to support Marcus, Priya, or David, do it. They earned it.
>
> I know this is hard. I am here, and so is the leadership team.
>
> Sarah

## What Changed (and Why)

| Element | Before | After | Why It Matters |
|---------|--------|-------|----------------|
| Names | "valued team members" | Marcus, Priya, David | Anonymity reads as cowardice. Naming people is the only respectful version. |
| Reason | "streamline operations" | Runway math + enterprise focus | People accept hard truths. They reject vague spin. |
| Severance | Not mentioned | 8 weeks, 3 months health, equity acceleration | The first question on every reader's mind. Answer it before they ask. |
| Timing | "Effective immediately" | Specific dates, AMA tomorrow, Thursday update | Vague timing creates rumor. Specific dates create calm. |
| Tone | Corporate apology language | Direct, human, unhedged | "Parting ways" is the phrase that breaks trust. Removed. |
| Compliance | Generic | Reviewed for state law on terminations | Severance and equity language must align with employment counsel. Flagged. |

## How the System Did It

The pipeline:
- Pre-flight: Read all 7 C-core files (about-me, company-context, project-brief, voice-dna, icp-profile, team-roster, company-glossary)
- Specialist: HR Specialist applied domain expertise on layoff communications, named the actual stakeholders from team-roster
- Voice: Copywriter matched the company's direct, low-corporate tone from voice-dna
- Compliance: Employment Law Reviewer flagged severance phrasing and confirmed state-specific termination language was correct
- Quality: Gatekeeper approved

The whole thing took 5 minutes instead of 2 hours.
