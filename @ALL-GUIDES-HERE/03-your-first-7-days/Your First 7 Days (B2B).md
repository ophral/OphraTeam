# Your First 7 Days (B2B Edition)

> If you're using this in a company context (HR, Finance, Legal, Product, Management, Support, or Customer Success), follow this 7-day path.
>
> If you're an individual creator, use the original `Your First 7 Days.md` instead.

---

## Day 1: Foundation (30 minutes)

**Goal:** Fill in the 7 core files so every agent has the context it needs.

### What to Do

1. Open `C-core/about-me.md` and fill in YOU as a person (role, what you own, goals, how you work, personal voice).
2. Open `C-core/company-context.md` and fill in the COMPANY (industry, stage, priorities, brand voice). You as a person are not the company. Both files matter.
3. Open `C-core/project-brief.md` and fill in what YOU produce in this role at this company (the intersection of about-me and company-context).
4. Open `C-core/voice-dna.md` and add notes on how you write professionally. Personal voice + brand voice.
5. Open `C-core/icp-profile.md` and describe who reads YOUR outputs (your team, board, customers, candidates, etc.).
6. Open `C-core/team-roster.md` and add the people you work with.
7. Open `C-core/company-glossary.md` and dump in 5-10 internal terms.

### Why This Matters

Every agent reads all 7 files before any task. This 30 minutes pays itself back the first time you create an output.

### Common Mistakes

- Mixing about-me and company-context. about-me is YOU. company-context is THE COMPANY. Keep them separate.
- Filling in `project-brief` like it's a marketing pitch. It's about what YOU produce, not the company's positioning.
- Skipping the glossary because "everyone knows what ARR means." They don't, your AI doesn't.
- Listing 50 names in the team-roster. Stick to the 10 you interact with weekly.

## Day 2: Pick Your Track (15 minutes)

**Goal:** Find your department in `T-tools/01-prompts/05-BONUS/_deluxe/` and run the first prompt.

### What to Do

1. Open `_deluxe/README.md` to find your department.
2. Navigate to your folder (e.g., `_deluxe/01-hr/`).
3. Run `01-learn-from-context.md` (copy-paste into your AI chat).
4. The agent will read your C-core and ask if anything needs clarification. Answer.

### Why This Matters

This is the agent calibrating itself to YOUR context before you ask it to do real work.

---

## Day 3: Add Samples (Optional, 20 minutes)

**Goal:** Drop 2-3 examples of YOUR real writing so outputs sound like you.

### What to Do

1. Open `B-brain/02-my-samples/_deluxe/[your-department]/`
2. Save 2-3 of your best past outputs as `sample-01.md`, `sample-02.md`, etc.
3. Run `02-learn-from-samples.md` from your track folder.
4. The agent reads your samples and updates voice patterns.

### Why It's Optional

The system works without samples. But adding them is the difference between "AI-flavored corporate" and "sounds like me."

---

## Day 4: Create Your First Output (45 minutes)

**Goal:** Use the system on a real task you have to do anyway.

### What to Do

1. Pick something real you'd write today (a JD, a board update, a SOP, whatever your role demands).
2. Open `03-create-output.md` from your track.
3. Fill in the prompt's [details] section.
4. Send to your AI chat.
5. The system runs the full pipeline (specialist, voice, compliance, gatekeeper).

### Quality Bar

The output should be 80% ready to send. You'll want to tweak the last 20%. That's normal and good.

### What If It's Bad?

Two likely causes:
1. Your C-core files are too vague. Go back, add specifics.
2. You skipped samples. Add 2-3 and re-run.

---

## Day 5: Run a Cross-Workflow (60 minutes)

**Goal:** Experience the killer feature: multi-department orchestration.

### What to Do

1. Pick a real business event you have coming up (hiring, feature launch, process change, QBR, etc.)
2. Open `T-tools/03-workflows/_deluxe/` and find the matching cross-workflow (numbered 01-07).
3. Tell Adam: "I want to run cross-workflow 01-hire-a-new-role for [the role]"
4. Adam orchestrates HR + Finance + Management agents. You get a bundle.

### Why This Is The Crown Jewel

Single-department outputs are useful. Multi-department coordinated bundles are unique. Nobody else has this. Most companies waste days on this kind of coordination.

---

## Day 6: Close The Loop (20 minutes)

**Goal:** Feed back what worked so the next output is better.

### What to Do

1. Use the output you created on Day 4 in real life.
2. Note what you changed before sending. Why?
3. Note any reactions you got.
4. Run `04-close-the-loop.md` from your track folder.
5. Paste in feedback. The agent updates `M-memory/learning-log.md`.

### Why This Matters

The system compounds. Every closed loop makes the next output sharper. Skipping this turns the system into a one-shot generator instead of a learning partner.

---

## Day 7: Review and Decide (30 minutes)

**Goal:** Honest assessment of what you got from the first week.

### Questions to Ask Yourself

- Which output saved the most time?
- Which output sounded most like you?
- What's still rough?
- Which agent or skill is missing for your role?

### What to Do With the Answers

- If something's missing: tell Adam ("/adam I need a [thing]"). Adam can add agents, skills, or workflows.
- If your voice still sounds off: add more samples to your track folder.
- If a particular output type was clutch: note it in `M-memory/learning-log.md` so you remember to use that prompt again.

### The Honest Test

After 7 days, you should be able to answer: "would I miss this if it disappeared?"

If yes: you're set. Keep using it.
If no: tell Adam what's broken. The system is meant to bend to your work, not the other way around.

---

## What Comes After Day 7

The 4-step rhythm becomes your default for any output:

1. Pre-flight (already happens automatically)
2. Create
3. Review and use
4. Close the loop

Add new skills, agents, or workflows whenever you notice a gap.
