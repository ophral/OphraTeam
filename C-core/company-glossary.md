# Company Glossary

## Terms
- **NBD:** New Business Development, Nadav's team.
- **Growth engine:** a meaningful, sustainable, repeatable source of growth. More than a product, a deal or a partnership.
- **Understand / Create / Build:** the three domains (Spatial / Engineering / Manufacturing).
- **Empowering the Next:** the company's strategic identity.
- **Business unit (BU):** existing unit that owns an established portfolio, its sales and support.
- **Transition and scale:** handover of a validated new business from NBD to a BU.
- **Physical AI / Physics AI / Engineering AI / Geospatial AI / Industrial AI:** the five AI fields Systematics tracks. Keep them distinct.
- **VAR:** value-added reseller. Other partner models: distributor, implementation partner, technology partner, services partner.

## Systems
- Salesforce: CRM, quotes, price books, pipeline, support tickets
- SAP Business One: ERP, item codes, costs, price lists, invoicing

## Style rules for Nadav's voice
- Prefer: build, win, launch, partner, close. "Here's the idea", "The upside is...", "Next step:", "Let's move on this".
- Banned: synergy, leverage, circle back, touch base, "I just wanted to", "perhaps", "it might be worth considering".
- Public content: no engineering jargon, no technical deep dives.
- Do not call AI a fourth business domain. It is a capability across all three.
- Do not present aspirations (such as 25% services) as targets or current performance.
