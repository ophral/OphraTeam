# Project Brief

## What I Produce
Communications, content and decision material for Systematics New Business Development, in my role as Business and Strategy Lead.

## The Intersection
**Who I am:** Nadav Leventer, leads NBD. Business voice between global technology vendors and companies that build physical things.
**Who Systematics is:** Israeli technology company helping organizations understand, create and build in the physical world, moving from product distribution toward trusted strategic partner.

## Output Types
1. **Vendor and partner outreach:** first contact, follow-ups, partnership proposals to technology vendors (VAR, distributor, implementation or services partner models)
2. **Opportunity material:** one-pagers and business cases that cover customer problem, target market, solution, value proposition, differentiation, revenue model, fit with Systematics
3. **Internal communication:** updates to leadership, business units, Finance and the NBD team; proposals for pricing and rollout (per business unit)
4. **Decision briefs:** pursue or drop an opportunity, vendor evaluation, prioritization
5. **Public thought leadership:** LinkedIn posts and articles on AI in the physical world: adoption, business models, where value lands, which industries move first
6. **Summaries:** meetings, vendor calls, customer conversations

## For Whom
- Technology vendors and startups (partners)
- Company leadership and business units (alignment)
- Founders, VCs, PE, industrial leaders (public audience)
- Customers in aerospace and defense, manufacturing, electronics, medical devices, public sector

## Why It Matters
NBD's success is new growth engines, not ideas. Sharp, fast communication moves opportunities from discovery to validation to business creation, and builds my public name as the face of where Systematics goes over the next 20 years.

## Working Rules
- Deciding not to pursue an opportunity is a valid, valuable outcome. Briefs should make that call possible.
- A promising vendor is not necessarily a promising business. Evaluate the whole opportunity.
- Pricing: propose, never decide (Finance plus BU). Sales and support: plan per business unit.
