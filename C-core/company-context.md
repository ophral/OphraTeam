# Company Context

## Name
Systematics Ltd. (Israel)

## What It Is
Established Israeli technology company serving organizations that work in the physical world. It brings leading international technology vendors to the Israeli market and adds the local expertise to select, implement and use them: software and technology distribution, partnerships, hardware, consulting, implementation, training and support.

## Strategic Identity: Empowering the Next
- **WHY:** To empower what's next in shaping the physical world.
- **HOW:** By challenging customers to embrace what's next in technology and workflows, and providing the solutions and expertise to achieve more.
- **WHAT:** Solutions and services across Engineering, Spatial and Manufacturing, helping customers understand, create and build.

## Ambition
Become the trusted strategic partner customers approach with a problem or initiative, before they know which product they need. Value flow: understand the challenge, define the outcome, set requirements, evaluate options, select, implement, adopt.

## Three Domains (AI cuts across all, it is not a fourth domain)
| Domain | Focus | Core technologies |
|---|---|---|
| **UNDERSTAND** | Spatial and geographic intelligence | Esri ArcGIS |
| **CREATE** | Engineering and product development | SOLIDWORKS, CATIA, ENOVIA, 3DEXPERIENCE, MATLAB, Simulink, Speedgoat, Altium Designer |
| **BUILD** | Manufacturing and production | HP 3D Printing, Formlabs, Markforged, Peel 3D, Tulip |

## Customers
Aerospace and defense, industrial manufacturing, electronics and technology, medical devices, government and public sector, infrastructure and utilities, research and academia, startups and deep tech.

## Business Model
Technology revenue (licenses, subscriptions, maintenance, hardware) plus professional services (consulting, implementation, training, ongoing support). Customer Success is being built. Aspiration, not an approved target: services at about 25% of business-unit revenue.

## Strategic Growth Priorities
1. Expand customer value across workflows
2. Build a diversified portfolio (less single-vendor dependence)
3. Grow professional services
4. Develop AI-based offerings (Engineering AI, Geospatial AI, Industrial AI, Physics AI, Physical AI)
5. Expand into adjacent markets
6. Create sustainable new sources of growth

## New Business Development (my team)
**Purpose:** Identify, develop and establish Systematics' next growth engines.
**Scope:** new technology products, new professional services, technology partnerships (VAR, distributor, implementation, technology or services partner), adjacent workflows, new segments and markets, new business models.
**Pipeline:** Identify → Validate → Develop → Establish → Transition and scale (the business unit takes over once ready).
**Boundaries:** not a general research team, not a proprietary product developer, not limited to AI, not hunting commodity products, not replacing business units, not the permanent owner of every new business.
**Growth engine test:** meaningful customer value, commercial potential, strategic fit, differentiation, repeatability, scalability, sustainability.
**Success:** discovery → validation (including deciding NOT to pursue) → business creation (new offerings, revenue, repeat demand). Numeric targets not yet defined.

## How the Company Operates (confirmed 2026-10-24)
- **Pricing:** set by Finance together with the relevant business unit. NBD proposes, does not set.
- **Sales and support:** not centralized. Each business unit has its own. Any process, training or launch is rolled out per business unit.
- **Systems:** Salesforce (CRM, product catalogue, price books, quotes, pipeline, support tickets). SAP Business One (ERP, item codes, costs, price lists, invoicing).
