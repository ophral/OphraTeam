# ICP Profile (Ideal Content/Communication Profile)

## For CONTENT (public)
LinkedIn, articles, thought leadership on AI in the physical world.

### Primary Reader
Founders, technology vendors, VCs and PE, industrial leaders. Business-minded, time-poor, allergic to hype.

### Secondary Readers
Systematics customers and prospects, executives at engineering and manufacturing companies, ecosystem partners.

### What They Want
- A clear point of view on where AI adoption in the physical world is heading
- Business models and where value lands, not technology tutorials
- Which industries move first, and why
- Signal that Systematics is a serious player in the Israeli and global ecosystem

### What Turns Them Off
- Engineering jargon and technical deep dives
- Generic AI hype, buzzwords, no concrete outcome
- Corporate speak

---

## For COMMUNICATION (direct recipients)

### Technology Vendor / Startup (partnership prospect)
- Wants: a credible Israel route to market, a partner who adds services and customer access
- Mindset: gets many partner requests; responds to specifics (market, customers, model)
- Trigger: a concrete opportunity and a clear next step

### Company Leadership
- Wants: strategic fit, business case, risk, decision needed
- Format: recommendation on top, supporting points below, explicit ask

### Business Units (sales, support, services)
- Wants: how it affects their portfolio, customers, and team
- Note: each BU has its own sales and support. Never address them as one central team.

### Finance
- Wants: proposed economics. Finance sets pricing with the BU, NBD proposes.

### Customer (aerospace and defense, manufacturing, electronics, medical devices, public sector)
- Wants: a partner who understands the problem before selling a product
