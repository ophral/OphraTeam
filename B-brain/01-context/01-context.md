## Company
Systematics Ltd. is an Israeli technology company serving organizations that work in the physical world. It brings leading international vendors to Israel and adds expertise: distribution, partnerships, hardware, consulting, implementation, training, support. Identity: **Empowering the Next**. Ambition: be the trusted partner customers call with a problem, before they know which product they need.

## Domains
- **UNDERSTAND** (spatial): Esri ArcGIS
- **CREATE** (engineering): SOLIDWORKS, CATIA, ENOVIA, 3DEXPERIENCE, MATLAB, Simulink, Speedgoat, Altium Designer
- **BUILD** (manufacturing): HP 3D Printing, Formlabs, Markforged, Peel 3D, Tulip
- **AI** cuts across all three. It is not a fourth domain.

## New Business Development
Purpose: identify, develop and establish Systematics' next growth engines.
Team: Nadav Leventer (Business and Strategy), Roni Peer (Technology), Gili Shmuel (Product and Offering). Shared ownership.
Flow: Identify, Validate, Develop, Establish, Transition and scale (to the business unit).
Opportunity test: meaningful customer problem, suitable solution, credible business for Systematics. Also strategic fit, differentiation, commercial attractiveness, repeatability, scalability.
Vendor evaluation: product, customers, market in Israel, portfolio fit, competition, vendor maturity, partnership model, local representation, services potential, economics, strategic value. A promising vendor is not necessarily a promising business.
