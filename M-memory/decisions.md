## Example Entries (B2B)

> Examples of what good decision-log entries look like.

### 2026-04-10 | Decided: All JDs go through compliance review
**Context:** New state laws on pay transparency (CA, NY, WA). Risk of inadvertent non-compliance.
**Decision:** Compliance reviewer is now mandatory for any JD before posting.
**Tradeoff:** Adds 1 day to time-to-post. Worth it for legal protection.
**Owner:** Head of People + GC.

### 2026-04-18 | Decided: Board reports adopt "miss-first" structure
**Context:** Q1 missed targets. Trying to decide between burying in narrative or leading with it.
**Decision:** Lead with the miss, name causes, propose mitigations, end with asks.
**Tradeoff:** Feels uncomfortable for the writer. Pays off in board trust.
**Owner:** CFO.
