## Example Entries (B2B)

> Examples of what good audience-feedback entries look like.

### 2026-04-15 | JD posted for Senior PM
**Audience:** Candidates from referrals + LinkedIn
**Signal:** 47 applications in 5 days (3x the previous role at this level). 12 from referrals (unusually high).
**Hypothesis:** "Remote-first" mention upfront + specific equity range made it more attractive than competitors who hide both.
**Action:** Continue including both upfront in all future JDs at this level.

### 2026-04-22 | Q1 Board Report sent
**Audience:** Board (5 members)
**Signal:** Two board members texted thanks for being direct. One asked for next quarter's report 3 days early.
**Hypothesis:** Leading with the miss built trust. Burying it would have been read as evasion.
**Action:** Adopt "lead with the variance" as standard for all board comms.
