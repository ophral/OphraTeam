### 2026-06-01 | System Update | New Agent Added: Market Researcher

**Agent file:** `A-agents/market-researcher-agent.md`

**Role:** Researches market landscape, finds comparable companies, sizes markets, and identifies key challenges. Provides the market intelligence layer that other agents build strategy and analysis on top of.

**How to invoke:** `/market-researcher [research request]`

**Works with:**
- Strategist — feeds market data into strategic analysis
- Devil's Advocate — provides the factual base for challenging assumptions
- Analyst — hands off raw market data for structured synthesis
- Chief of Staff — contributes market context to decision briefs

**When to use:**
- Before entering a new market or vertical
- When sizing TAM/SAM/SOM for a pitch or board report
- When identifying comparable companies (comps) for benchmarking or positioning
- When mapping key challenges and competitive dynamics in a space

**Promote to voice-dna?** No — agent addition, not a voice pattern.

### 2026-04-15 | HR Track | Job Description for Senior PM

**What worked:**
- Specifying "remote-first" upfront in C-core meant the JD didn't need editing on this dimension
- Adding 3 sample JDs to `_deluxe/01-hr/` made the voice immediately match
- Compliance reviewer caught "must have leadership experience" as exclusionary, suggested "demonstrated influence at scale"

**What didn't:**
- Initial draft was too long (700 words). Recruiter trimmed to 400. Future: target 400 from the start.

**Promote to voice-dna?** Yes: "JDs at our company aim for 350-450 words, max 5 must-haves, max 3 nice-to-haves."

### 2026-04-22 | Finance Track | Q1 Board Report

**What worked:**
- Leading with the miss (instead of burying it on page 4) got faster decision-making in the meeting
- Using the company-glossary "ELG" instead of "enterprise late-stage" matched how the board talks
- Adding "asks of the board" section turned the report from informational to actionable

**What didn't:**
- The "next quarter forecast" was too rosy. CFO pushed back. Future: build in 15% buffer for downside risk.

**Promote to voice-dna?** Yes: "Board reports lead with the variance, name specific deals/causes, and end with 1-3 explicit asks."
