# Prompt: Learn From Context (THINKING track)

Copy and paste this into your chat:

---

```
Read my answers in B-brain/01-context/01-context.md. 

Then split everything across the 5 core files. One pass. No editing agents or skills.

Route the content this way:
- about-me.md         ← Part A of 01-context.md (the person)
- company-context.md  ← Part B (the company)
- icp-profile.md      ← Part C (the audience). For CONTENT, weight toward public readers (newsletter, social, blog), not internal recipients.
- voice-dna.md        ← A8 (personal voice) + B9 (brand voice) + any voice signals you pulled from the sample files. For CONTENT, weight BRAND voice heavily. Note where personal and brand differ.
- project-brief.md    ← intersection of A and B (what I produce in this role at this company, for whom, and why).

If 01-context.md mentions team members, named products, or banned phrases, also drop those into team-roster.md and company-glossary.md. Otherwise leave those two alone. Skip api-keys-registry.md.

Never invent. If a section in 01-context.md is blank AND no samples cover it, leave `[TODO: fill in]` in the target file.

Show me, in short lines:
1. Me (one line)
2. Company (one line)
3. Audience (one line)
4. Voice (one phrase I actually say + one I'd never say)
5. Files I filled
6. ONE clarifying question that would sharpen the next pass.
```
