# Prompt: Learn From Samples (THINKING track)

Copy and paste this into your chat:

---

```
Read EVERY sample across ALL folders inside B-brain/02-my-samples/. That means 01-content/, 02-communication/, 03-thinking/, and any other subfolder I added. Skip README files and empty templates.

Classify each non-empty sample yourself by reading it:

- CONTENT: publishable to a wide audience. Social posts, newsletters, blog posts, articles, marketing copy.
- COMMUNICATION: a message to a specific person or team. Emails, Slack, DMs, internal updates, client replies.
- THINKING: a decision document or analysis. Briefs, memos, OKR docs, strategy notes, options analyses.
- OTHER: clearly none of the above (poetry, journal, code, recipe). Note it but do not learn voice from it.

I picked the THINKING track, so weight your learning toward THINKING. But learn from every non-empty sample. Cross-domain patterns reveal how I reason and persuade.

For each domain that has samples, learn TWO things:

A. **Communication style.** Tone, sentence structure, formality, words I use and avoid, language mix.

B. **Thinking and decision-making style.** How I organize information. How I structure a document. How I present a decision (recommendation first or evidence first?). How much detail. How I handle uncertainty. How I weigh data vs intuition. Risk tolerance signals.

Then update exactly 3 files. Nothing else. Do not touch other agents or skills.

1. C-core/voice-dna.md: add or replace TWO sections per domain that had samples:
   - `## Patterns from [DOMAIN] samples: communication style (learned [today's date])`
   - `## Patterns from [DOMAIN] samples: thinking and document style (learned [today's date])`
   Cite at least one sample filename per pattern. Bullets, not prose.

2. A-agents/chief-of-staff-agent.md: add or replace a section `## Owner's Brief Preferences (THINKING focus, cross-domain aware)`:
   - Where the recommendation lives (top? bottom?)
   - TL;DR first or evidence first
   - Length preference (one-pager vs full brief)
   - Tables vs prose vs bullets
   - Data vs intuition balance
   - How I want trade-offs presented (steelman both sides? best/worst case?)
   - "When in doubt, structure like sample-XX.md (from [folder])"

3. M-memory/learning-log.md: append one dated entry. Include: how many samples per domain, classification surprises (samples in wrong folder), top communication AND thinking patterns, what to watch for next.

Show me, in 6 short blocks:
- Classification (one line per sample: filename, where it sits, what it actually is)
- Top 5 thinking and document patterns (with sample citation)
- Top 3 communication patterns
- One pattern that surprised you
- Files updated (3 lines)
- ONE clarifying question.
```
