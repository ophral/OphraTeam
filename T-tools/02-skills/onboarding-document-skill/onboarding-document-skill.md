# Skill: Onboarding Document

> Creates 30/60/90 plans and welcome materials that set new hires up for real success.

## Trigger Words
"onboarding", "new hire", "first day", "30/60/90", "welcome kit"

## Format

```markdown
# Onboarding Plan: [Role Title]

**Date:** [Date]
**Type:** 30/60/90 Onboarding Plan
**New Hire:** [Name]
**Manager:** [Name]
**Start Date:** [Date]
**Buddy:** [Name]

---

## Welcome

[2-3 sentences: genuine welcome. What excites us about this hire joining.]

---

## First 30 Days: Learn

**Goal:** Understand how things work here. Ask every question.

| Week | Focus | Key Activities | Success Looks Like |
|------|-------|----------------|--------------------|
| 1 | Orientation | [Specific activities] | [Observable outcome] |
| 2 | Shadow | [Specific activities] | [Observable outcome] |
| 3 | Assist | [Specific activities] | [Observable outcome] |
| 4 | Own small task | [Specific activities] | [Observable outcome] |

**Key people to meet:**
- [Name, Role: why they matter to your work]
- [Name, Role: why they matter to your work]
- [Name, Role: why they matter to your work]

---

## Days 31-60: Contribute

**Goal:** Start adding value independently. Make your first real impact.

| Week | Focus | Key Activities | Success Looks Like |
|------|-------|----------------|--------------------|
| 5-6 | [Focus area] | [Specific activities] | [Observable outcome] |
| 7-8 | [Focus area] | [Specific activities] | [Observable outcome] |

**First real project:** [Specific, achievable project with clear deliverable]

---

## Days 61-90: Own

**Goal:** Operate independently. Identify what you want to improve.

| Week | Focus | Key Activities | Success Looks Like |
|------|-------|----------------|--------------------|
| 9-10 | [Focus area] | [Specific activities] | [Observable outcome] |
| 11-12 | [Focus area] | [Specific activities] | [Observable outcome] |

**90-day milestone:** [What "success" looks like at 90 days. Be specific.]

---

## Resources

- [Tool/system: link and access instructions]
- [Documentation: where to find what]
- [Communication norms: how the team works]

---

## Check-in Schedule

- **Daily (Week 1):** 15-min with buddy
- **Weekly (Month 1):** 30-min with manager
- **Bi-weekly (Months 2-3):** 30-min with manager
- **Day 90:** Formal review conversation
```

## Quality Checks

- [ ] Goals are observable (not "get comfortable with")
- [ ] Activities are specific enough to actually do
- [ ] Success criteria are measurable
- [ ] First project is achievable (not overwhelming, not trivial)
- [ ] Resources include actual links and access instructions

---

## Top-1% Knowledge

What separates a great practitioner of this format from an average one. Use these as the quality bar.

- **The 30-60-90 Frame.** What will success look like at 30, 60, 90 days. Specific, measurable.
- **The Three-Lists.** What I should KNOW by day 30. What I should BE DOING by day 60. What I should BE LEADING by day 90.
- **The Stakeholder Map.** Who to meet, in what order, why. The reverse-org-chart approach.
- **Pre-Day-One Material.** What new hires should read before showing up. Sets expectations and reduces day-one anxiety.
- **The Buddy System.** Every new hire pairs with a peer (not manager) for the first 60 days. Cultural transfer happens here.

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

The predictable failure modes for this format. Check against these before shipping.

- Information dump on day one. New hires retain 10% of what they hear in week one.
- No success criteria. New hire wonders what "good" looks like for weeks.
- Treating onboarding as HR's job. It's the manager's job. HR sets the stage.
- Same onboarding for every role. Engineer onboarding looks different than CSM onboarding.
- No 30-day check-in. By the time the 90-day review happens, retention windows have closed.
