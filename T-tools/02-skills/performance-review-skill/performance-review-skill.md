# Skill: Performance Review

> Creates structured review templates and self-assessments that drive genuine development conversations.

## Trigger Words
"review", "performance", "feedback cycle", "self-assessment", "annual review"

## Format

```markdown
# Performance Review: [Employee Name]

**Date:** [Date]
**Type:** Performance Review
**Period:** [Q1 2025 / H1 2025 / Annual 2025]
**Role:** [Current title]
**Manager:** [Name]
**Review Type:** [Self-Assessment / Manager Review / 360]

---

## Summary Rating

**Overall:** [Exceeds / Meets / Developing / Below]
**Trajectory:** [Accelerating / Steady / Stalling]

---

## Goals from Last Period

| Goal | Target | Actual | Rating |
|------|--------|--------|--------|
| [Goal 1] | [Measurable target] | [What happened] | [Met/Exceeded/Missed] |
| [Goal 2] | [Measurable target] | [What happened] | [Met/Exceeded/Missed] |
| [Goal 3] | [Measurable target] | [What happened] | [Met/Exceeded/Missed] |

---

## Strengths (with evidence)

1. **[Strength]:** [Specific example, date, impact]
2. **[Strength]:** [Specific example, date, impact]
3. **[Strength]:** [Specific example, date, impact]

---

## Growth Areas (with support plan)

1. **[Area]:** [Why it matters] / **Support:** [What we'll provide] / **Target:** [Observable improvement]
2. **[Area]:** [Why it matters] / **Support:** [What we'll provide] / **Target:** [Observable improvement]

---

## Goals for Next Period

| Goal | Metric | Timeline | Support Needed |
|------|--------|----------|----------------|
| [Goal 1] | [How we'll measure] | [By when] | [Resources/training] |
| [Goal 2] | [How we'll measure] | [By when] | [Resources/training] |
| [Goal 3] | [How we'll measure] | [By when] | [Resources/training] |

---

## Career Development

**Current level:** [Level]
**Next level:** [Level]
**Gap to close:** [Specific skills or scope needed]
**Timeline estimate:** [Realistic timeframe]

---

## Employee Comments

[Space for employee to respond, agree, or add context]
```

## Quality Checks

- [ ] Every strength has a specific example (not just adjectives)
- [ ] Growth areas include support (not just "do better")
- [ ] Goals are measurable (not "improve communication")
- [ ] Rating is justified by the evidence above it
- [ ] Tone is developmental, not punitive

---

## Top-1% Knowledge

What separates a great practitioner of this format from an average one. Use these as the quality bar.

- **SBI (Situation-Behavior-Impact).** Anchor every feedback point in observable behavior, not personality traits.
- **The Two Lists.** Two things to keep doing, two things to start doing, two things to stop doing. Concrete and actionable.
- **Forward-Weighted Reviews.** 30% backward (what happened), 70% forward (what's next). Backwards-only reviews demotivate.
- **Calibration Before Delivery.** Multiple managers compare ratings before delivery. Prevents grade inflation and unfair variation.
- **The Self-Assessment First.** Employee writes their own assessment before manager writes theirs. Reveals gaps in perception.

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

The predictable failure modes for this format. Check against these before shipping.

- Generic praise. "Great team player" tells the person nothing useful.
- Bad-news surprises at review time. Feedback should be continuous, not annual.
- No specific examples. "You need to be more strategic" without a moment to anchor.
- Same template for everyone. Top performers and struggling performers need different reviews.
- No growth path. Review without development plan is just judgment.
