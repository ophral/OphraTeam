# Skill: Contract Summary

> Creates plain-language contract summaries that non-lawyers can actually understand and act on.

## Trigger Words
"contract summary", "agreement review", "contract review", "plain language summary", "legal summary"

## Format

```markdown
# Contract Summary: [Agreement Name]

**Date:** [Date]
**Type:** Contract Summary (Not Legal Advice)
**Agreement:** [Full name of contract]
**Between:** [Party A] and [Party B]
**Summarized by:** [Name]
**Original length:** [X pages]
```

## Quality Checks

- [ ] No legal advice given (summary and flags only)
- [ ] Risks are flagged by severity (not all treated equally)
- [ ] Plain language throughout (no legalese echoed without translation)
- [ ] Section references allow quick lookup in original
- [ ] "What it means for us" is practical, not theoretical

## Top-1% Knowledge

What separates a great practitioner of this format from an average one. Use these as the quality bar.

- **The 80/20 of risk.** Most contract risk lives in 5 clauses: indemnification, liability cap, IP, termination, governing law. Surface those first.
- **The Business-Reader Format.** Plain English summary up top. Legal detail below. Most readers stop at the top.
- **Risk Tier the Findings.** Critical (must change), Important (negotiate), Acceptable (live with). Three tiers, not five.
- **The Comparison to Standard.** "Indemnification cap is $500K vs our standard $5M." Anchors the deviation.
- **The Counterparty Lens.** What would the other side push back on, and where would they fold? Negotiation map.

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

The predictable failure modes for this format. Check against these before shipping.

- Summarizing every clause equally. Reader can't tell what matters.
- Legalese throughout. The whole point of summary is translation.
- Missing the omissions. What ISN'T in the contract often matters more than what is.
- No business impact. "This clause is unusual" without "and here's what it means for us."
- No recommendation. The summary describes. A useful summary recommends.
