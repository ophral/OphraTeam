# Skill: Job Description

> Creates structured, inclusive job descriptions that attract the right candidates and set honest expectations.

## Trigger Words
"job description", "JD", "hiring", "role posting", "open position"

## Format

```markdown
# [Role Title]

**Date:** [Date]
**Type:** Job Description
**Department:** [Department]
**Level:** [Junior / Mid / Senior / Lead / Director]
**Location:** [Location / Remote / Hybrid]
**Reports to:** [Title]

---
```

## Quality Checks

- [ ] Must-haves are genuinely required (not aspirational)
- [ ] No gendered language (he/she, rockstar, ninja, etc.)
- [ ] Years of experience are reasonable for the level
- [ ] Bullets describe impact, not just tasks
- [ ] Nice-to-haves are truly optional (not stealth requirements)

---

## Top-1% Knowledge

What separates a great practitioner of this format from an average one. Use these as the quality bar.

- **Outcome-Based, Not Responsibility-Based.** "Drive 30% YoY revenue growth in EMEA" beats "Responsible for revenue growth in EMEA." Specific outcomes attract specific candidates.
- **The 90-Day Test.** What will success look like at day 90? Specific, measurable. Filters self-aware candidates.
- **Must-Have vs Nice-to-Have.** Five must-haves max. The list signals what you actually care about. Long lists signal you don't know.
- **Anti-resume Language.** Describe what they'll DO, not what credentials they need. Resumes are filters, not predictors.
- **Salary Transparency.** Posted ranges attract serious candidates and filter out time-wasters. The data is clear.

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

The predictable failure modes for this format. Check against these before shipping.

- Copy-paste from old JDs. Drift accumulates. Posting reads like nobody owns it.
- Buzzword soup. "Rockstar," "ninja," "wear many hats." Tells candidates the company doesn't know what it needs.
- Endless requirements list. 25 must-haves filters out everyone except people who lie on resumes.
- No salary range. Top candidates filter past this in the first scroll.
- Generic culture statements. "We value diversity" is table stakes. Show, don't claim.
